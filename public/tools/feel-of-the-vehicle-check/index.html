Feel Of The Vehicle Check

A one-page demo check that sells how the car feels, not just what it has, using the cabin lighting most consultants skip. Includes a 15-minute rollout for your next sales meeting.

The demo playbook

Five moves. Do not skip the silence.

1. Set the mood
Goal: let them see the light

DoPull the car into a shaded bay if it is bright out, or wait for dusk for the delivery. Do not just talk about the lighting. Show it.

2. Light it, then stop talking
Goal: three seconds of silence

DoTurn the cabin lighting on. Give the customer three seconds to sit in it. Their brain does the work.

3. Ask the feeling question
Goal: connect the glow to an emotion

Say"This glow right here. How do you feel right now?" Then: "Remember that feeling. After a long day you'll step into your car and exhale. You made the right call."

4. Hand them the dimmer
Goal: co-ownership

Say"What color makes you feel super chill?" Change it to their pick. They realize they can shape the car, and they start a relationship with this specific vehicle.

5. Anchor it
Goal: a line they remember

Say"This isn't just light. This is what it feels like when your day is done. The world shuts up and this car says, 'Welcome back.'", it lands harder than "and we have dual zone climate control."

Carry it through the visit

Small habits that keep the feeling alive.

Next vehicle

If you show a second car, set its lighting to the color they chose in the first. The transition feels familiar.

Used cars

Check the used units. If one has ambient lighting, show it. It signals that someone cared for the car.

Test drive

When conversation goes quiet and their eyes glaze a little, mental ownership is taking hold. Do not interrupt it.

Delivery

Set the lighting to how the customer loves it before they drive off, so their first night drive feels like the showroom.

Manager rollout: 15 minutes

Run it in this week's sales meeting, ideally on the lot.

  • Minutes 0 to 3. Tell the dome light story. Ask: what are the things on our cars we show only when a customer asks, or never?
  • Minutes 3 to 6. Pick two demo vehicles. Each consultant finds the ambient and footwell lighting controls and the available colors.
  • Minutes 6 to 12. Pairs run the five-move playbook in the car. The partner times the three seconds of silence and notes whether the feeling question was asked.
  • Minutes 12 to 15. Agree that every demo vehicle gets its lighting set before it is pulled around. Set the date you will do a ride-along check.

Weekly inspection

Inspect the behavior, not the result. Survey comments about how the car felt are the lagging number.
