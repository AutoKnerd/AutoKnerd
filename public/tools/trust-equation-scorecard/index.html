The equation

Empathy plus trust equals a sale. Empathy is the spark. Trust is the fuel.

Empathy (free)

Listening, caring, asking smart questions, and asking "why." Looking at the world through the customer's lens.

Your CRM and desking tool cannot measure this.
Trust (earned)

Following through. Admitting what you do not know and then showing up with the answer, including at delivery.

Lead with kindness: it takes down anxiety and stress.

Three one-liners that start trust

Not a script to recite. A starting point in your own words.

Discovery
Goal: find what actually matters

Say"Just so I'm clear, what's most important to you in your next vehicle?" Get the top five to seven things, then find the hot button and why it is a hot button. You sell those things, not the whole car.

Mid-conversation
Goal: show you will not leave them hanging

Say"Let me walk you through what I know, and I'll flag anything I'm not sure about so I can get you the answer." Keep a notepad in your back pocket and write the questions down.

After delivery
Goal: extend the trust

Say"Would it be okay if I checked in with you tomorrow to make sure everything made sense?" It tells them you are not letting them fall, and a redelivery is fine if they need one.

Talk-time rules of thumb

Floor rules that put trust to work.

Under 45%

Consultants who talk less than 45% of the time have higher win ratios.

90 / 10

Andrew's rule: 90% of the conversation about the customer, 10% about you.

85 / 15

If it is about the product, 85% about the customer's life and 15% about the product.

Manager rollout: 15 minutes

Run it in this week's sales meeting.

  • Minutes 0 to 3. Read the 95% and 61% numbers. Ask: what is the last thing a customer said about us that had nothing to do with the car?
  • Minutes 3 to 6. Write "empathy plus trust equals a sale" on the board. Split the room into spark and fuel and list two behaviors under each.
  • Minutes 6 to 12. Pairs role-play the three one-liners. The partner scores the table above and counts how much of the time the "customer" talked.
  • Minutes 12 to 15. Check your lead response: who is answering web leads personally within one minute? Set the day you will review scorecards.
